How to Hang Jumbo Cookie Wall Art: A Complete Guide to Faux Food Decor

Learn the best techniques for hanging oversized cookie sculptures safely and beautifully. From choosing the right hardware to creating gallery wall arrangements with faux food wall art.

Erynn

September 1, 2024

So you've fallen in love with a piece of jumbo cookie wall art — a handmade, oversized faux food sculpture that looks good enough to eat. Now comes the fun part: getting it on your wall where everyone can admire it. Whether you're hanging a single statement piece or creating an entire faux food wall art gallery, this guide has everything you need.

Unlike heavy commercial food props that can weigh 10+ pounds and require serious hardware, handmade cookie sculptures from artists like those at JOIRUSH are specifically designed to be lightweight and hangable — typically just 2-4 pounds. That means no special anchors, no contractor visits, and no stress.

Before You Start: What You'll Need

Gathering your supplies first makes the whole process smoother. For most faux food wall art installations, you'll need:

  • Picture hanging hook or nail — rated for at least 10-20 lbs
  • Hammer — for driving in the hook
  • Level — nobody wants a crooked cookie
  • Pencil — for marking your spot
  • Measuring tape — for precise placement
  • Stud finder (optional) — if you want extra security

Step 1: Choose the Perfect Location

The right spot can transform a great piece into a showstopper. When selecting where to hang your jumbo cookie wall art, consider:

  • Lighting: Natural light brings out the realistic texture and paint details. Avoid direct sunlight that could fade colors over time.
  • Visibility: Think about where you and your guests will see it most — above a breakfast nook, in a hallway, or as a kitchen focal point.
  • Conversation factor: These pieces are meant to spark joy and start conversations. Put them somewhere people will naturally gather.

Popular spots for faux cookie sculptures include kitchens (of course), dining rooms, breakfast nooks, bakery-style cafés, content creator studios, and playrooms. The classic chocolate chip cookie looks stunning against white or light-colored walls, while colorful M&M cookie versions pop against neutral backgrounds.

Step 2: Check the Weight Rating

One of the biggest advantages of handmade cookie wall art over commercial fiberglass props is the weight difference. A typical JOIRUSH jumbo cookie weighs around 2-3 pounds — light enough for a standard picture hook, sturdy enough to stay put.

As a rule of thumb, use hardware rated for at least double the actual weight of your piece. So for a 3-pound cookie, a 10-lb rated hook gives you plenty of margin for safety.

Step 3: Mark Your Position

Before you put any holes in the wall, figure out exactly where you want your piece. Here's the easiest method:

  1. Have someone hold the cookie against the wall while you step back and check the placement
  2. Use painter's tape to outline the position if you need to try different spots
  3. Once you've decided, make a light pencil mark at the top center of where the cookie sits
  4. Use a level against your mark to make sure it's straight

Pro tip: For the most natural look, hang the center of your artwork at eye level — about 57-60 inches from the floor. In kitchens, you might go slightly higher to account for counters and cabinets.

Step 4: Find the Hanger Height

Every JOIRUSH cookie comes with a built-in wall hanger on the back. You need to know how far down from the top of the piece that hanger sits:

  1. Turn your cookie over and find the hanger (usually a sawtooth hanger or D-ring)
  2. Measure from the very top of the cookie down to the hanger point
  3. Subtract that distance from your wall mark — that's where your hook goes

For example, if your mark is at 60 inches and the hanger is 2 inches from the top, you'll install your hook at 58 inches.

Step 5: Install the Hook

Now for the satisfying part — putting that hook in place:

  1. If you're near a stud, drive directly into it for maximum hold
  2. For drywall, angle your picture hook slightly upward as you hammer it in
  3. For heavier pieces or extra peace of mind, use a drywall anchor first
  4. Make sure the hook is level and secure before proceeding

Step 6: Hang and Admire

The moment of truth! Carefully lift your jumbo cookie sculpture and hang it on the hook. Use your level one more time to check the positioning. Step back, take a photo, and enjoy watching everyone do a double-take when they see it.

Creating a Gallery Wall with Multiple Cookies

One cookie is a statement. Multiple cookies? That's a dessert gallery wall — and it's one of the most impactful ways to display your collection.

Here's how to plan a cohesive arrangement:

  1. Start on the floor: Lay your pieces out and experiment with different arrangements. The M&M cookie set is specifically designed to be displayed as a pair.
  2. Consider color flow: Mix classic cookies with colorful candy versions for visual interest, or stick to a monochromatic theme.
  3. Space them evenly: 3-6 inches between pieces creates cohesion without crowding. Larger gaps (8-12 inches) work for a more relaxed, organic feel.
  4. Use painter's tape: Cut out paper templates the size of each cookie and tape them to the wall before committing to holes.

Special Considerations for Different Rooms

Kitchens

Kitchens are the natural home for faux food wall art, but keep these tips in mind:

  • Avoid hanging directly above the stove — heat and grease can affect the finish
  • Great spots: above the breakfast bar, beside the fridge, or on a blank wall visible from the dining area
  • The rainbow candy cookie adds perfect pop to white kitchen cabinets

Cafés and Bakeries

For commercial spaces like bakeries and cafés, consider:

  • High-traffic visibility — place where customers can photograph them
  • Groupings that tell a story about your menu
  • Secure mounting since spaces see more movement and vibration

Content Creator Studios

For photoshoot and video backdrops:

  • Position where they'll appear in frame without being distracting
  • Consider how lighting affects the painted surfaces on camera
  • Bold pieces like the hot pink M&M cookiephotograph beautifully

Frequently Asked Questions

How heavy is jumbo cookie wall art?

Most handmade jumbo cookie sculptures weigh between 2-4 pounds, making them lightweight compared to commercial fiberglass props that can weigh 10+ pounds. This makes them easy to hang with standard picture hooks — no professional installation needed.

What type of hook do I need for faux food wall art?

A standard picture hanging hook rated for 10-20 lbs works perfectly for mostjumbo cookie wall art. For drywall without studs, use a hook with a built-in anchor for extra security. Command strips are not recommended for pieces over 2 pounds.

Can I hang cookie wall art in a kitchen?

Absolutely! Kitchens are one of the most popular spots for cookie wall art. Just avoid hanging directly above the stove where heat and grease could affect the acrylic paint finish over time. Above a breakfast nook or on a blank wall works perfectly.

How do I create a gallery wall with multiple cookie sculptures?

Start by laying out your pieces on the floor to find an arrangement you love. Use painter's tape or paper templates to mark positions on the wall before committing. Spacefaux cookie sculptures 3-6 inches apart for visual cohesion, and use a level to keep everything straight.

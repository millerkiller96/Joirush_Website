Frequently Asked Questions

Is this cookie art edible?
No, this is decorative cookie art: a wall sculpture made from spray foam and acrylic paint. These art cookies are designed to look like real sugar cookies but are not food. They're faux food wall art meant to last forever on your wall.
What is cookie art?
Cookie art refers to decorative sugar cookie wall sculptures, handmade faux food wall art that looks like real cookies. Unlike edible sugar cookie art, these pieces are crafted from durable materials by a cookie artist and designed to hang on your wall permanently.
How long does shipping take?
Each cookie art piece ships in 14 days. Every sugar cookie wall sculpture is handcrafted to order by cookie artist Erynn. The 14 day timeline includes handmade production and shipping. True artistry takes time.
How do I hang this cookie canvas on my wall?
Each cookie art piece comes with an attached wall hanger on the back. Simply use a nail or picture hook rated for the weight (most pieces weigh 2 to 4 lbs). These sugar cookie wall sculptures are lightweight and easy to hang.
How big is this art cookie?
Most jumbo cookie art pieces are approximately 16 inches in diameter. Check the specific listing for exact dimensions of each sugar cookie wall sculpture.
Do you ship cookie art internationally?
Currently we offer free U.S. shipping with 14 day handmade production and delivery. For international cookie art orders, please contact us through the custom order form for a shipping quote.
Can I commission custom cookie art?
Absolutely! Visit our custom orders page to commission sugar cookie wall sculptures in specific colors, sizes, or flavors. Cookie artist Erynn loves creating one of a kind art cookies.

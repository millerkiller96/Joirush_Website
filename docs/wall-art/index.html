Why These Hit Different

No factory. No fiberglass blob. Each cookie is hand-sculpted by Erynn in Orlando — one at a time, made for your wall.

Factory props cost $350+ and weigh 10+ pounds. These? $90-$225, 2-4 lbs, and you can actually hang them on a single nail. Plus they look better because an actual artist made them.

The process takes days: sculpting spray foam texture layer by layer, building up acrylic paint, positioning each chip by hand, sealing for durability. The result is cookie wall art that makes people reach out to touch it.

Perfect for a dopamine decor kitchen, a foodie gift they'll never forget, or that blank wall that needs something joyful.

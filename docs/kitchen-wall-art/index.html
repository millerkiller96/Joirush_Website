Placement guide

Where to Hang Kitchen Wall Art

Kitchens are busy rooms. These spots show off art and keep it out of the way of heat, steam, and splashes.

Breakfast nook

The easiest spot of all. It works like a mini dining room, so one cookie at seated eye level feels cozy.

Coffee or snack bar

A cookie over the coffee station is a little joke everyone gets. Hang it high enough to clear the machine.

End of the cabinets

The wall at the end of a cabinet run is often seen from the doorway. Great for a statement piece.

Above the table

A pair or the ice cream sandwich centered over a table or banquette anchors the whole room.

Skip: right above the stove, the splash zone around the sink, and anywhere a cabinet door could bump it. Steer clear of strong direct sun too, to keep the acrylic colors bright. For coffee bars, skinny walls, rentals, and the space above the cabinets, see our kitchen wall decor ideas by spot.

Sizing

What Size Works on Your Wall?

A quick rule: the art should feel generous for the wall it sits on, not lost on it.

  • Narrow wall or between windows: one 14 inch cookie, like the rainbow candy cookie.
  • Open wall or nook: a 16 inch classic, like the chocolate chip.
  • Over a table or banquette: a pair, like the M&M cookie set, or the ice cream sandwich.

Make It Part of the Room

Common questions

Kitchen Wall Art FAQ

What kind of wall art is best for a kitchen?
Pick art that suits the room's mood and can handle a busy space. Food themed art is a natural fit. Hang it on a wall away from the stove and sink, such as a breakfast nook, coffee bar, or the end of a cabinet run.
Where should I hang art in my kitchen?
Good spots include above a dining table or banquette, beside a window, over a coffee or snack station, or on an open wall you see from the doorway. Avoid the area right above the stove and the splash zone around the sink.
How big should kitchen wall art be?
On a narrow wall between cabinets or windows, a single 14 inch cookie looks right. On a wider wall or above a table, try a 16 inch piece, a pair, or the ice cream sandwich statement piece.
Are JOIRUSH cookies real food?
No. They are decorative only, sculpted from spray foam and painted with acrylics. They look like real cookies but are not edible.
How do they hang?
Each cookie has a built in hanger, and most weigh 2 to 4 lbs, so they hang on a single nail or picture hook.
